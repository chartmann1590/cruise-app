Guest Wi-Fi Chat — no app, no internet, no refresh

One phone hosts a local-only hotspot + tiny web server. Guests join the Wi-Fi and chat in any browser. Captive-portal auto-opens like hotel Wi-Fi.

How it works

Two QR codes, 30 seconds

Host taps Start Guest Chat in Party. Phone creates a local hotspot (no internet) on :8085 and shows:

  1. Step 1 — Join Wi-Fi: scan WIFI:T:WPA;S:<ssid>;P:<password>;; QR or enter manually
  2. Step 2 — Open chat: scan URL QR or type http://<host-ip>:8085/ — OS captive-portal check (generate_204 / captive.apple.com) auto-opens the page; no typing if you use the QR
Text-only2000 charsOne hostLive WebSocketHistory on join

One shared thread: app ↔ host ↔ browser via PartyChatRepository + ChatWebServer (ws://<host>:8085/ws). Guest name (40 chars) + localStorage UUID → PartyMember. Host messages fan-out via onMessagePersisted; guest messages → receiveFromWebGuest → Nearby relay.

Stay live: WebSocket timeout=0 + 25s ping keepalive, debounced offline dot, history merge — no manual refresh. Organized Party screen: collapsible Connections & members, Bluetooth Nearby, Guest Wi-Fi, Party Members, Tools above chat, chat at bottom above composer.

How it works

Three steps and you're set

You can get ready before you board, then use it the whole trip.

1

Create your cruise

Name your ship and choose your travel dates. We'll lay out the days for you.

2

Add ports and plans

Search for Nassau or Cozumel and add it to your trip. Then add dinners, excursions and reminders to any day.

3

Use it anywhere

Check the weather once in port and view it later at sea. Chat over Bluetooth, or host a Guest Wi-Fi — guests join your hotspot via QR and chat in their browser, no app needed. No internet, no refresh.
