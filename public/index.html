Features

Made for life on board

Simple tools for the parts of a cruise that matter most.

🗓️

Plan your days

Create your trip, set your dates, and build an itinerary for every sea day and port day. Add dinners, excursions and reminders in a few taps.

📍

Add port stops

Search for a city and add it to your route. We fill in the location for you so you can keep planning.

🌤️

Weather when you need it

Check the forecast for any port — then view it later without signal. It stays available for a few hours after you check.

⏰

Reminders

Set a reminder for anything on your itinerary and get notified at the right time, even after you restart your phone.

💬

Chat with your group

Stay in touch with your party without needing ship Wi-Fi. Share a QR code to connect — it works nearby over Bluetooth.

📆

Easy to use

A clean calendar and simple screens help you get set up quickly, so you can get back to enjoying the trip.

How it works

Three steps and you're set

You can get ready before you board, then use it the whole trip.

1

Create your cruise

Name your ship and choose your travel dates. We'll lay out the days for you.

2

Add ports and plans

Search for Nassau or Cozumel and add it to your trip. Then add dinners, excursions and reminders to any day.

3

Use it anywhere

Check the weather once in port and view it later at sea. Chat with your group nearby — no internet needed.
