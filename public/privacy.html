2. What stays on your device (offline-first)

The app is offline-first. Room database cruise_db v3 is the source of truth and lives only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to our servers — because we run none.

  • Cruise: ship name, start/end dates (midnight-normalized).
  • PortStop: name, latitude/longitude, arrival/departure, country, orderIndex.
  • PlannedEvent: title, dateMillis, startTimeMillis, location, category, reminderMinutesBefore (1–60), description.
  • PartyMember: displayName, isSelf, persistent code (UUID), endpointId, stored in cruise_party_prefs via SharedPreferences.
  • Message: clientMessageId (UUID), senderName, text, timestamp, isFromSelf, status (PENDING→SENT→DELIVERED→READ), targetCode/targetName, retryCount. Chat text is P2P only.
  • WeatherCache: portStopId, fetchedAt, json blob, tempMax/Min, weatherCode, summary — 3h TTL.

Fallback is fallbackToDestructiveMigration(). You can clear data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Cruise Planner → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall. We do not back up your Room DB to Google backup by default beyond the standard allowBackup:true — disable backups if you prefer.

ℹ️
No mock data: Fresh install shows Welcome Aboard! → Create Cruise only. DebugInjectorActivity is an exported test helper that injects demo “Island Princess” data only when you launch it with extras — not auto-run.

4. Open-Meteo weather & geocoding

When you add a port or view weather, the app calls:

  • https://geocoding-api.open-meteo.com/v1/search?name={your text}&count=5 — to resolve city → lat/lon
  • https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude={lat}&longitude={lon}&daily=temperature_2m_max,...&current=temperature_2m,weather_code&timezone=auto&forecast_days=7 — no apikey param

This is a direct request from your device to Open-Meteo. We do not proxy or log it. Your query text and lat/lon are sent to Open-Meteo per their privacy policy and CC BY 4.0 attribution (shown in WeatherCard.kt). They may log IP & request. Pull once on port Wi-Fi, then the response is cached 3h in WeatherCache for offline sea days. Block via firewall or skip weather if you prefer not to share location.

5. Nearby Connections — offline party chat

Party chat uses Google Play Services Nearby Connections Strategy.P2P_CLUSTER — an encrypted peer-to-peer mesh over Bluetooth + BLE + Wi-Fi Direct. No internet, no server, ~100m per hop.

  • Advertising/discovery name is "{displayName}|{code}" or JSON {"n":name,"c":code}. Peers see your chosen display name only.
  • Payloads are WireMessage serialized via kotlinx.serialization, sent via Payload.fromBytes(). Receipts are DELIVERED/READ.
  • Messages are stored in Room Message with retryJob every 5s until delivered. No message leaves Bluetooth/Wi-Fi Direct range.
  • We do not relay chats through any server. Google's Nearby stack handles encryption; see Nearby Connections overview.

QR identity: Your QR is generated locally via ZXing QRCodeWriter (no upload). Scanning adds PartyMember with that code. No contacts are uploaded.

8. Retention & deletion

  • Local Room: until you clear app data or uninstall. No remote copy.
  • SharedPreferences cruise_party_prefs: same — clearing removes self_code/self_name.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Google retains crash data ~90 days then aggregates. Request deletion by uninstalling and emailing contact to request removal of associated crash logs by installation ID (provide your UUID from Settings if exposed in future update).
  • Performance: trace aggregates retained per Firebase defaults.
  • Open-Meteo: per their retention; we don't store their logs.

9. Security

Local DB uses Android app-private storage. Nearby Connections is encrypted by Google Play Services. Weather uses HTTPS. Firebase uses TLS. No app-level encryption of Room at rest in v1.0 — device encryption (Android file-based) is relied upon. Report issues via GitHub or contact email.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It is for travelers planning cruises (general audience). If you believe a child’s data was sent via Crashlytics (e.g., a display name containing a child's name you typed), uninstall and contact us to request deletion.

11. Your rights & choices

  • Withdraw consent: Uninstall or deny permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, CAMERA, NEARBY). In v1.0 there is no in-app Crashlytics toggle — we plan to add one; until then, network-blocking (disable internet for the app) prevents uploads.
  • Access / correction: All your cruise data is in-app and editable (add/edit/delete events, ports, messages, party members).
  • Export / deletion: Clear storage or uninstall deletes everything locally. For Firebase crash logs, email us with your self_code (Settings → long-press QR) and we will request deletion from Firebase console.
  • Do Not Track: No tracking across sites; we ignore DNT headers.
  • Region: No geo-specific logic in v1.0; GDPR/CCPA rights as above. Data may be processed in US (Google).
